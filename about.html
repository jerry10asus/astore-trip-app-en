About AStore Trip

AStore Trip is a travel and collection app built for people who love Apple’s design language.

I made it because I believe every Apple retail store is a space where the company’s aesthetic shines as much as the products on display.

As a designer, I love visiting Apple Stores when I travel—taking in the architecture, materials, and how each one fits its city. Every location feels like a conversation between Apple and local culture, and a reminder that design is the experience. AStore Trip is meant to be a companion for anyone who feels the same way about Apple’s spaces.

With this app you can:

  •  Browse Apple Store locations worldwide with names, regions, building notes, and hours
  • ❤️ Save stores you’ve visited or want to visit and build your own Apple travel map
  • 🏅 Earn visit achievements and badges for every store you check in to

AStore Trip isn’t only a utility—it’s a way to connect travel, design, and what Apple stands for.
